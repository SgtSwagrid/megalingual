object LanguagePicker

A picker of languages: a button showing the chosen one, opening a list of those offered, each with its flag and its names, varieties beneath the languages they are varieties of, under a box searching them.

A picker of languages: a button showing the chosen one, opening a list of those offered, each with its flag and its names, varieties beneath the languages they are varieties of, under a box searching them.

It is an ARIA listbox, searched by a combobox above it that keeps the focus while the list is open, so that whatever is typed is searched for. The list keeps the languages any of whose names, in any language, holds what was typed, ignoring case and accents. Arrow keys, Home and End move through it, Enter chooses, and Escape, Tab or a click elsewhere closes it. A letter typed on the button opens the list, searching for it.

The interface's language and the label are signals, so that the picker stays where it is, and keeps the focus, when the interface changes language.

Its classes all begin megalingual-. Its stylesheet is added once, first in the page's head, its rules each of one class, so that they outlast element resets (Tailwind's, Bootstrap's) and any later rule of the app's overrides them. It does not reach into a shadow root. Its colours are custom properties: --megalingual-text, -surface, -border, -accent, -active, -radius and -shadow, by default the system's.
